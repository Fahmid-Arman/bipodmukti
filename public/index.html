"I am safe." From any phone, with no internet.

When floods and cyclones hit, mobile data fails first and families panic. BipodMukti lets a person check in from a basic phone with one USSD dial or one SMS. Their family gets an SMS at once. If someone needs help and nobody replies, BipodMukti moves to the next family member, then to local volunteers, until a person confirms they are handling it.

Try it on the phone simulator Open the coordinator console

1. Check in

Dial *213*55# and press 1 for SAFE or 2 for HELP, or send BM SAFE / BM HELP rescue to 21213. Works on any Banglalink phone.

2. Family is told

Up to three family contacts, in order. Each help alert has a short reference like H42. The contact replies BM OK H42 to say "I am handling it".

3. Nobody is forgotten

No reply in time, or the SMS fails? The next contact is alerted, then local volunteers. After a warning, coordinators run a roll call for a whole area and see who has not answered.

Built on Applink

Applink APIWhat BipodMukti uses it for
USSDCheck-in menu on basic phones: SAFE, HELP (rescue, medical, food), moving to shelter, family contacts, join and leave. One-dial shortcut: *213*55*1*1# = safe at home.
SMSHelp alerts, acknowledgements, roll calls and receipts (MT); replies like BM OK H42 (MO); delivery reports to skip a contact whose phone is off.
SubscriptionOpt-in with recorded consent, opt-out with BM STOP, a local mirror fed by subscriber notifications, base size as a health check.
OTPWeb sign-up for family members and NGO staff: verify the number and activate the subscription.
